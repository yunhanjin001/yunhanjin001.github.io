About Me
======

I am currently pursuing a Master’s in Financial Mathematics at Columbia University, after completing my undergraduate studies in Finance at Fudan University. Through internships at several hedge funds, I discovered my deep interest in uncovering alpha in the markets and expressing it through rigorous research and modeling. In these experiences, I continues to improve key skills for quantitative research, including:

- Intuition for interpreting diverse market information
- Proficiency in data processing, vectorized computation, and algorithm design
- Application of statistical models and market structure analysis

If you’re also interested in Alpha Research and Optimization, I’d love to connect and exchange ideas. Even if your focus is different, I always enjoy meeting new people and learning from diverse perspectives.
